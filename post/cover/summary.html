Real-time streaming:
Spark 4.3 RTM vs Flink 2.3

The stateless tail is fixed. State is the new story.
Same pipeline, same 7 cores / 14 GB, S3 checkpoints every 60 s. Stateless at 100k/s, stateful at 50k/s.
Spark 4.3 RTM Apache Flink 2.3

1. Stateless latency: now a tie

p99 at 100k events/s. Both medians are ~6 ms.
4.3 moves offset commits off the data path (async progress tracking): Spark's p99 drops 21x.

2. Stateful latency: Flink's tail is 2.8x lower

p99, 10 s windowed aggregation per user, 50k events/s, both engines tuned.
Median 6.9 vs 13.4 ms. Dedup: same gap. Sessions with timers: Spark can't hold 50k/s (0.76x).

3. CPU: similar average, Spark hits its limit

Peak engine cores, windowed aggregation at 50k/s (mean 3.5 vs 3.9). Stateless: 1.1 vs 1.7 cores.

4. Memory: Flink runs on ~2/3 of Spark's

Engine working set, GiB.
Most of the gap is JVM heap: peak 3.2 vs 6.2 GiB.
Stateless: either engine works. Stateful: Flink is faster and leaner.
